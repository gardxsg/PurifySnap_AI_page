# Privacy Policy for PurifySnap AI

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026

PurifySnap AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

### 1. 100% On-Device Data Processing
PurifySnap AI is an on-device utility application. All text recognition (OCR), screenshot scanning, category mapping, and auto-rule processing occur strictly locally on your physical device. 

- We **DO NOT** upload, transmit, store, or back up your screenshots, photos, or extracted text to any external server or cloud service.
- We **DO NOT** collect personal identifying information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

### 2. Device Permissions
To provide core app functionality, PurifySnap AI requests access to your device's photo library/storage. This permission is strictly used to scan and organize your screenshots locally.

### 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Our app integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Ads). Google AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage data (such as device type, IP address, and advertising ID) to serve policy-compliant ads. 

For more details on how Google uses your data, please review:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

### 4. Children’s Privacy
PurifySnap AI does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
