# Privacy Policy for PurifySnap AI

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026  

PurifySnap AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information when you use PurifySnap AI on Android devices.

---

## 🔒 1. 100% On-Device Local AI & Multi-Script OCR Processing

PurifySnap AI is built with a strict **Privacy-First, On-Device Architecture**:

* **Zero Cloud Uploads:** All photo scanning, Google ML Kit multi-script OCR text extraction (supporting English, Hindi, Devanagari, European, and Asian scripts), custom folder filtering, and language translation run **100% locally on your phone**.
* Your personal photos, bank receipts, UPI payment proofs, ID cards, and chat screenshots are **NEVER uploaded to any cloud server or third-party service**.

---

## 📱 2. Device Permissions Requested

To provide screenshot organization and storage cleanup features, PurifySnap AI requests the following local permissions on your device:

* **Storage & Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** Required solely to fetch gallery screenshots and user-specified custom folder images for local OCR categorization and storage reclamation.
* **Notifications Permission (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Optional permission used to notify you when daily auto-cleanup scans complete or storage limits are reached.

---

## 🛡️ 3. Privacy Exclusion Filters & User Controls

We provide full control over your private files and data:

* **Excluded Folders:** You can specify sensitive storage folders (e.g., "Vault", "Private") to bypass scanning completely.
* **Excluded Keywords:** You can define confidential keywords (e.g., "OTP", "Password", "PIN") to skip matching screenshots from auto-mapping.
* **30-Day Recycle Bin:** Soft-deleted screenshots remain in your local Recycle Bin for 30 days before permanent auto-purging, allowing 1-tap instant restoration anytime.

---

## 📊 4. Advertising & GDPR / CCPA Privacy Consent

PurifySnap AI uses Google AdMob for displaying non-intrusive advertisements to support app development:

* **Google AdMob & UMP SDK:** We integrate Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to gather explicit privacy consent choices in compliance with GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA privacy laws.
* **Firebase Analytics:** We collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable app usage analytics and crash logs to improve app performance and stability.
* You can manage or revoke your personalized advertising choices anytime via **Settings > Ad Privacy & Consent Settings**.

## 🔐 6. Data Security & Retention

Since all core features run locally on your device, PurifySnap AI does not maintain user accounts or remote databases. Uninstalling the application will immediately erase all local application preferences and Recycle Bin records from your device.
